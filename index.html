Hurmizyar OÜ builds software with a purpose: preserving and promoting Kurdish culture and language, and creating practical SaaS tools for companies in Kurdistan and Europe.

About

Hurmizyar OÜ is an independent software company registered in Estonia, operating from the EU. We build focused, well-crafted products rather than chasing trends.

Our work is guided by two commitments: using software to preserve and carry Kurdish culture and language forward, and building practical tools that help companies in Kurdistan and Europe do their work better.
